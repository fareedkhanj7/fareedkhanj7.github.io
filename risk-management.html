Essential Risk Management & Position Sizing Rules for Traders

Published by Fareed Market Research Team • 5 Min Read

Every professional trader understands a fundamental truth: you cannot control what the market does next, but you have 100% control over how much you risk on any single trade. Even the most accurate technical indicator setup will experience losing streaks due to sudden macroeconomic news, liquidity sweeps, or unexpected volatility.

The Golden 1% to 2% Rule

The cornerstone of sustainable trading is limiting your exposure on any individual position to no more than 1% to 2% of your total account balance. For example, if you are trading with a $500 balance, your maximum risk per position should remain between $5 and $10. This ensures that even a rare streak of five consecutive losses only reduces your equity by 5% to 10%, leaving you mentally calm and financially able to recover.

Why Martingale & Revenge Trading Destroy Accounts

Many inexperienced traders fall into the trap of doubling their trade size after a loss (known as the Martingale system) in an attempt to win back lost money immediately. While this may work temporarily, a single extended trend will wipe out weeks of gains in minutes. Always stick to fixed or percentage-based position sizing and set a strict daily stop-loss limit (such as stopping for the day after 3 losses).
