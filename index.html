How Our Technical Analysis Engine Works

Our live market terminal connects directly to public WebSocket feeds for cryptocurrency pairs and real-time institutional reference rates for major foreign exchange pairs. The chart renders real-time candlesticks overlaid with two Exponential Moving Averages: a fast 9-period EMA (cyan) and a slower 20-period EMA (amber). Simultaneously, the engine computes a 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI) to evaluate whether current price momentum is overextended.
